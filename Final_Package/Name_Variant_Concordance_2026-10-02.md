## 1. Why the names are separated

Agencies and museums match the string on the paper, not the string the family uses. A search for "Rodheim" alone returns a Bavarian place name. A search for "Seymour Rodheim" returns the captured German report and the missing-air-crew file. "Rod" and "S.R." do not appear as catalog headings. This note records each form exactly, says whether it is on an official file or only in family use, and gives the path to the original.

## 3. The five name forms

### Rodheim

Surname only. Too broad to identify the man. Use it to find the file, then confirm with the controls above.

- Kept: MACR 4874, NAID 90970052, RG 92, series Missing Air Crew Reports, HMS/MLR A1 2109B. Catalog text does not print the crew list on the description page. Aircraft serial on the description is 42-39966. Departure is AAF Sta 105, APO 557. https://catalog.archives.gov/id/90970052
- Kept: KU-763A, titled "Rodheim, Seymour," NAID 142686000, RG 242, series Downed Allied Aircraft Reports, HMS/MLR A1 1024, Box 361. Digitized PDF: `7290268_Box361_FolderKU763A.pdf`. https://catalog.archives.gov/id/142686000
- Rejected: NAID 669730381, an aerial photograph whose caption contains the place name Rodheim, Bavaria. Not a person.
- Rejected: other Rodheim and Rotheim naturalization and cemetery entries that do not match 1921–1966 and the 305th.

### Seymour Rodheim

This is the form on the official wartime paper that is online.

- Exact phrase search on catalog.archives.gov returned three archival descriptions. The two that match the controls are MACR 4874 and KU-763A. The catalog heading of the captured report is "Rodheim, Seymour," surname first.
- American Air Museum person page is headed "Seymour Rodheim," linked to the 305th Bomb Group, 366th Bomb Squadron, aircraft 42-39966, mission 351 on 11 May 1944, event text "Bailed out of aircraft 42-39966 near Saarbrucken." The page cites MACR 4874 via Fold3. It is an index, not the report. https://americanarchive.iwm.org.uk/archive/person/seymour-rodheim
- Sons of Liberty Museum page is headed "Seymour Rodheim" and gives POW camp Stalag Luft 3 Sagan-Silesia Bavaria, moved to Nuremberg-Langwasser, 49-11, returned to military control, 305th heavy bomber. It cites the U.S. National Archives. The page title carries serial 426037851. That serial does not match O-757851 in the family request. See section 5.

### S.R. Rodheim

No official catalog heading, American Air Museum heading, or POW-index page was found under "S.R. Rodheim" or "S. R. Rodheim" on 2 October 2026. Treat this as a family and filing abbreviation only. Do not put it on an eVetRecs or museum accession form as the veteran's name. If a morning report or Form 5 uses initials, attach that page when it is in hand and quote the initials from the page.

### Rod Rodheim

Spoken name, not a record name. The repository README states that he did not like to be called Seymour and introduced himself as Rod, and that genealogical records sometimes reverse the given names. No National Archives file unit is titled "Rod Rodheim." Use "Rod" in narrative and oral-history notes. Use "Seymour Rodney Rodheim" on every form that asks for the service name.

### Rodney Rodheim

This form appears in civilian indexes, not in the wartime catalog hits checked today.

- AncientFaces surname compilation lists "Rodney Rodheim," born 3 November 1921, died March 1966, Granada Hills, California. That date pair matches the family file. It is a compiled index, not a vital record.
- A Find a Grave index search result lists "Rodney Seymour Rodheim," born 3 November 1921, died 2 March 1966, Glendale, Los Angeles County. Given-name order is reversed from the service form. Confirm against the death certificate before citing the cemetery entry as identity proof.
- The family repository already records both orders: Seymour Rodney as the service name, Rodney Seymour as the form he used because he did not want to be called Seymour.

## 5. Serial number conflict — do not smooth it

Two numbers are in circulation. They are not the same string, and this note does not pick a winner.

- Family repository request, `Requests/2026-09-04_eVetRecs_O757851.md`: officer serial O-757851. That file also says the loss file is MACR 4874, not MACR 7702.
- Sons of Liberty Museum page, transcribed from a National Archives POW extract: 426037851, printed in the page title as `rodheim-seymour-r426037851`.

The next original that settles this is the POW index card or the OMPF, not another website. Request both numbers in the comments field of the eVetRecs order so the archivist can search the aliases. Quote the card when it arrives. Until then, lead with O-757851 and footnote 426037851 as an unverified transcription.

## 6. Paths to the original paper

### Digitized now

- MACR 4874 description: https://catalog.archives.gov/id/90970052. RG 92, A1 2109B. Unrestricted. Scanned in the Ancestry partnership and also on microform publication M1380, fiche 01740. The description page is not the crew list. Open the images and read the AFPPA-14.
- KU-763A, "Rodheim, Seymour": https://catalog.archives.gov/id/142686000. RG 242, A1 1024, Box 361. Unrestricted. PDF filename `7290268_Box361_FolderKU763A.pdf`. This is the captured German downed-aircraft report folder, the closest online original to a handwritten enemy record of the loss.

### Not digitized as a named file — request path

- Official Military Personnel File. Start at https://vetrecs.archives.gov/. Name: Seymour Rodney Rodheim. Also search Rodney Seymour Rodheim. Serial: O-757851, and ask them to check 426037851. Branch: Army Air Forces. Comment: complete OMPF, including reconstructed documents from the 1973 fire, for a formal awards package and museum donation. Upload proof of death.
- Morning reports for the 366th Bomb Squadron, May 1944, are in the unit morning-report series, requested through the National Archives. Ask for 11 May 1944 and the following days, 366th Bombardment Squadron, 305th Bombardment Group, Station 105.
- Stalag Luft III camp lists are RG 389. Ask for the officer roster entry under Rodheim, Seymour, and the 1945 evacuation lists.

## 8. Filing rule for the package

On the tab of every copy, write the name as it appears on that document, then the control name in brackets. Example: `Rodheim, Seymour [Seymour Rodney Rodheim] — KU-763A, NAID 142686000`. Do not retitle a scan to a name the paper does not use. The concordance is the crosswalk. The scan stays honest.
