## Result in one paragraph

Record Group 18 does not have a file unit titled with any of these eight strings. The paper that would name him sits inside a unit-arranged series, World War II Combat Operations Reports, and that series is not digitized under his name. The three extra spellings, Radheim, Rodhime, and Radheem, return catalog hits, but those hits are full-text or OCR matches in other record groups. None is a 305th Bomb Group personnel file, and none is in RG 18.

Scope, quoted from the series description: mission reports, operations and field orders, statistical summaries, and narrative and intelligence reports of Army Air Forces combat units. Arrangement, quoted: by category of unit (group, squadron, etc.), thereunder by type of unit (bomber, fighter, troop carrier, other), and thereunder numerically by individual unit.

That arrangement is why a name search fails. The 305th Bombardment Group (Heavy) reports are filed under the group number, not under Rodheim. The cataloged samples in the same series are other groups: 306th Bombardment Group Operations, Aug. 16–31, 1943, NAID 596344, Box 638; and several 2nd Bomb Group mission-report file units (NAIDs 2867052–2867057). An exact search for "305th Bombardment Group Operations" returned no catalog results on 2 October 2026. Absence from the catalog is not absence from the boxes.
