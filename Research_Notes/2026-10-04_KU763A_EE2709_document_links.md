# Rodheim document link register
Read 4 October 2026. Pulled from NARA Catalog media paths and set beside the family KU-763A photographs.

## What the three pulls decided

1. KU-763A (NAID 142686000) is the file in the family photographs. Six-page image scan. Dulag Luft card: bailed out 11 May 1944, 19:00, place entered Metz, Fortress, Seymour Rodheim, Chicago, Ill., 2.Lt., O-757851, gefangen, Dulag-Luft, card closed 21 June 1944, clerk Wa. Form S 5857/44. Companion page is the Gendarmerie Diedenhofen (Thionville) report, Kommandantur A 2/XII, addressed toward Dulag Luft Oberursel, about 17 May 1944, received 23 May 1944, red KU stamp. Text: an American airman was taken over from the gendarmerie; Seymour Rodheim, Lt., Erk.Nr. 0757851; interrogation produced little; further particulars could not be given.

2. E&E 2709 (NAID 5557325) is the navigator, not Rodheim. 2nd Lt Norbert J. Stefaniak, O-709069, 366th Bomb Squadron, 305th Bomb Group. IS9 (WEA) report 6/649/7504, interviewed 21 November 1944 by Theodore M. Purdy, evasion graded No. 5. He was an evader for six months, not a man who went straight to Dulag Luft. Narrative: mission to Saarbrücken 11 May 1944; Lt. Seewer ordered bailout (pilot spelling is OCR from the scan; confirm on MACR 4874); landed near Laumesfeld, northern Lorraine, quite near Thionville; walked southwest through Sierck and Rettel; hidden at Huntingen by August Long, 15 Huntingen, about 15 May to 10 November 1944; returned with a U.S. patrol to 90th Division. He does not name Rodheim in the narrative pages read. The two men separated at bailout. His landing place confirms the German capture district on KU-763A.

3. Parent KU-763 and lettered siblings (KU-763B / C) are not separate catalog file units. A Catalog search for KU-763 returns only KU-763A, Rodheim, Seymour. Request the parent and any sibling by number from RG 242, A1 1024 and A1 1022.

## Request text for the missing KU siblings

KU-763A is already cataloged as NAID 142686000, Box 361, Seymour Rodheim, O-757851, 11 May 1944. The sibling, if it exists, may be 2nd Lt Norbert J. Stefaniak, O-709069, 366th Bomb Squadron, 305th Bomb Group, same aircraft 42-39966, MACR 4874.
